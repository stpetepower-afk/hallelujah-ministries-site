Program

Job Creation & Career Pathways

Real skills, real employer connections, and a track record that opens doors — built for people who are ready to work.

Get in touch about this program

What this program includes

  • Job readiness training and coaching
  • Skills training aligned to local employer demand
  • Employer partnerships and warm introductions
  • Ongoing support through the first 90 days on the job

Who it's for

Individuals and families in Pinellas County working toward stability who are ready to engage in a structured, accountable program. Space and eligibility are determined case by case — reach out to start a conversation.
