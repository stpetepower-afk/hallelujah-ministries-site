Our Mission

A Storehouse for the Whole Person

Hallelujah Ministries is a storehouse in the biblical sense — a place to come and gather what you need to rebuild. Room and board, job creation, a path to owning your own home, economic development, holistic living, and micro-farming: practical resources, held together by care that never runs out.

"Bring the whole tithe into the storehouse, that there may be food in my house." — Malachi 3:10
— Hallelujah Ministries
